What is Coast FIRE?

It's the point where your investments have grown large enough that, even if you never contribute another dollar, compound interest will grow your portfolio to your full Financial Independence (FI) number by traditional retirement age.

How is it calculated?

We use your SWR and annual spend to find your FI Number. We then discount that future FI Number back to today using your expected real return rate. If your current balance > Coast Number, you are Coast FIRE!
