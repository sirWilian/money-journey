What is Coast FIRE?

The point where your investments have grown large enough that, even with zero additional contributions, compound interest will grow your portfolio to your full FI number by retirement age.

How is it calculated?

We use your SWR and annual spend to find your FI Number, then discount it back to today using your real return rate. If your current balance > Coast Number, you've already coasted!
